# Student and parent manual

## Signing in
Use the e-mail and password from the school. Change the password in **Account** after the first sign-in.

## Student
* **Dashboard**: today's lessons, recent marks, attendance and notifications.
* **Diary**: the week with lessons, marks and attendance marks per day. Use the arrows to change the week.
* **Marks**: all marks by subject and term, the average and the final mark of each subject (five-point scale, each mark has its own colour).
* **Attendance**: percentage and list of absences for a chosen period.
* **Timetable**: weekly schedule.
* **Export CSV**: the button on the marks and attendance pages downloads your data for Excel.

## Parent
After signing in choose a child at the top of the dashboard; every page then shows that child's diary, marks and attendance. You see only the children linked to you by the school. You get a notification when a new mark is posted or the child is marked absent.

## If something is wrong
* "Access denied" means the page is not available for your role.
* Wrong mark or attendance: contact the class teacher.
* Forgot the password: ask the school administrator for a reset.
