\chapter*{Declaration on the Use of Artificial Intelligence Tools}

\medskip
I hereby declare that during the preparation of my thesis I have used the following artificial intelligence-based tools.

\section*{1. Use of AI tools}

\fbox{\rule{0pt}{1.1ex}\rule{1.1ex}{0pt}}\hspace{0.3em}I used AI tools as follows:

\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Claude (Anthropic):} assistance with writing and understanding the project code, with writing and running tests and verification runs, with the conversion of the application to the five-point marking scheme, the diary-style interface and the news home page, and with drafting and revising the thesis text. Claude was used through Claude Code and the Claude application; several Claude models were used over the course of the work.
  \item No other artificial intelligence-based tools were used.
\end{itemize}

\section*{2. Declaration of independent work}

I declare that I take full responsibility for the professional content of my thesis. I acknowledge that the use of artificial intelligence-based tools does not replace independent work; therefore, I have verified all statements, results, interpretations, conclusions and references included in the thesis.

I further declare that:
\begin{itemize}
  \item the final content of the thesis was compiled by me,
  \item any AI-generated or modified content has been reviewed, verified and corrected where necessary,
  \item the original results, interpretations and conclusions presented in the thesis are my responsibility,
  \item the use of AI tools has been truthfully and fully disclosed in this declaration.
\end{itemize}
