# Teacher manual

## Signing in
Use the e-mail and password from the administrator. Change the password in **Account**.

## Dashboard
Shows today's lessons, classes whose attendance is not marked yet and the latest notifications. The bell in the top bar shows unread notifications.

## Marking attendance
1. **Attendance**, choose the class and the date (today by default).
2. Set a status for each student: present, absent, late, excused. Add a remark if needed.
3. **Save**. Parents of absent students are notified. If attendance is not marked by 15:00 you get a reminder.

## Recording marks
1. **Gradebook**, choose the class and the subject (only your own subjects are offered).
2. Choose the student, the mark **1-5** and the term (Q1-Q4, Midterm or Final), then **Add**. Other values are rejected.
3. A wrong mark is removed with **Delete** in the same table and entered again.
The subject mark of a student is the average of the marks rounded half up (4.5 becomes 5). Parents and the student are notified about each new mark.

## Reports
* **Attendance statistics**: per student and class totals and percentages for a period.
* **Export CSV** on the gradebook and statistics pages downloads the table for Excel.

## Timetable
**Timetable** shows your week. Changes are made by the administrator.
